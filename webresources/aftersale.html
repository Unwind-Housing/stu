    This page opens on two lists: the sales closed in the last 30 days, and every sale with after-sale tasks still to do, whenever it closed. Every closed sale is under Sale, the ones with work left first. The tasks are your organization's own list: whoever looks after Stu can change it under Settings, After-sale tasks. A sale gets its copy of the list the first time it is opened here, so a later change to the list doesn't alter sales already under way. Entering the buyer's insurance also records the home's first insurance verification, dated the closing, so the next one falls due a year after the purchase.
