    Received means the household's online form has come in: open the request to read the answers, then save your review. Issue found means you looked and something is wrong: it stays open until the verification is approved. Open a request to see the link that was sent, add the signed form, save your review or cancel it. Any open request can be saved as paper, even one sent as an online form. Approving a request sets the occupancy's Last occupancy verification date. Cancelling an approved one takes that date back to the verification before it.
