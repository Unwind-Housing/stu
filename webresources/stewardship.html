Occupancy verification

Occupancy verification falls due on each anniversary of the purchase (the occupancy's start date), whenever the last one came back. A new owner isn't due until the first anniversary. A verification counts for an anniversary when it came back no more than 60 days before it, or any time after. A home has a tick box when its verification is overdue or due soon and the household hasn't been asked yet. Untick any you don't want in this batch, then select Start occupancy verifications. Last contact is the newest relationship note or verification that came back.

Insurance runs on its own clock, separate from occupancy verification. A home is on this list when no insurance verification has been entered for it in the last 12 months. For a new owner the first one is due a year after the purchase. Entering the insurer's card, or approving proof a household sent, takes it off. The date used is the newest of the one typed on the occupancy and the ones on its records (insurance verifications approved, insurance policies verified).
