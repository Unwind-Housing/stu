    Price and monthly cost what a buyer pays, for each home size

    Stu works out the mortgage payment from the sale price, the down payment and the interest rate, then adds the other monthly costs to get the monthly housing cost. The pre-screen divides that by each applicant's income to test whether they can afford the home.

    The loan

    Interest rate by income band, if lower-income buyers can get a lower rate

    BuyersInterest rate (%)

    One band is enough when every buyer gets the same rate. Add a band when a loan program gives households below an income line a lower rate, or when homes are priced by the buyer's income.

    Each home size

    Every home of the same size gets the same price and costs. You can change one home, or a band's figures, on the project afterwards.

    Who fits which home which household sizes can apply for each home size, and in which tier

    Tier 1 ranks ahead of tier 2 on that home size's list. Leave a box blank and households of that size can't apply for that home size.

    The starting point: at least one person per bedroom and no more than two is tier 1; one person fewer than the bedrooms is tier 2. Change it to match your own policy.

    Review and create

    Invitation settings (how many households to invite, how long they have to respond) start at their usual values. You can change anything on the project afterwards.
