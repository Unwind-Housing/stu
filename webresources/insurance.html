The insurer's card

When your organization is listed as additional insured, the insurer mails a card. Enter it here: it is saved as an approved insurance verification for that home, with no need to ask the household.

Ask households for proof

Every current occupancy. Homes with no insurance verification in the last year (or, for a new owner, in the first year after the purchase) are ticked for you. Starting a request records that you asked, then gives you an email for each household to copy into your mail program. Stu does not send email itself. A card you entered shows here as Approved, with Insurer's card under How (tick the box above to see closed ones). Requested means the household has been asked and you are waiting for its proof. Open one and select Email to copy to get its email again, for a reminder. The wording of the email is your organization's own: whoever looks after Stu can change it under Settings, Message templates (the one used for Insurance verification). Issue found means you looked and something is wrong: it stays open until the insurance is approved. Open a request to add or view the document, save your review or cancel it. Approving one sets the occupancy's Last insurance verification date. Cancelling an approved one takes that date back to the approval before it.
