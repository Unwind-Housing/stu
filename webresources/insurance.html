    Requested means the household has been asked. To review means proof of insurance is waiting for you to look at. Needs contact means the household asked for help, or sent no proof. Issue found means you looked and something is wrong: it stays open until the insurance is approved. Open a request to see the link that was sent and the document, save your review or cancel it. Approving one sets the occupancy's Last insurance verification date. Cancelling an approved one takes that date back to the approval before it.
